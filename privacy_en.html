Clopass Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 11, 2026

Banakai ("we", "us", or "our") provides the mobile application "Clopass" (the "App"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect information in connection with the App.

1. Provider

Provider: Banakai
Contact: banakai.support@gmail.com

2. Information We Collect

The App collects and processes the following information:

  • User Identifier: When you launch the App, an anonymous authentication session is created and an internal user ID is automatically assigned. We generally do not collect your name, email address, or other information that directly identifies you.
  • User Content: Notes, paragraphs, fill-in-the-blank settings, test results, learning history, stamp records, and other content that you create, enter, paste, or import using the camera.
  • Camera Images: When you use the OCR (Optical Character Recognition) feature, captured images are temporarily transmitted to an AI service provider to extract text. We do not store the images on our servers.
  • Temporary Device Transfer Data: Temporary transfer codes and passwords used when transferring your data to another device. They become invalid after they expire or after they have been used.
  • Usage Data: The number of times certain features, such as camera OCR, are used in order to manage free usage limits.
  • Technical Information: Server error logs and other operational data used to maintain and improve the App. This information does not directly identify you.

The App does not use advertising SDKs or analytics SDKs.

3. How We Use Information

  • To provide App features, including note management, tests, learning history, and device transfer.
  • To provide study reminders through local notifications.
  • To maintain, troubleshoot, and improve the App.
  • To prevent misuse and manage usage limits.
  • To respond to your inquiries.

4. Camera and Images

When you use the OCR feature, captured images are transmitted to an AI service provider solely to extract text. We do not store the images on our servers. Only the text extracted through OCR is saved as part of your notes. The handling of your data by third-party service providers is governed by their respective privacy policies.

5. Third-Party Services

We use the following categories of third-party services as necessary to provide the App.

  • Cloud infrastructure, authentication, and database service providers.
  • AI service providers that extract text from images.

We do not disclose your information to third parties except as required by law.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

Data you create in the App is retained until you delete it.

Temporary device transfer codes and related information become invalid after they expire or after they have been used.

Note-sharing codes remain valid until you generate a new sharing code.

If you have questions about deleting your data, please contact us. We may be unable to fulfill certain requests due to identity verification requirements or technical limitations.

7. Your Rights

You may contact us to request deletion of your data. Please use the contact information below.

8. Security

We implement reasonable and appropriate safeguards to protect the information we collect from unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or destruction.

9. Children's Privacy

The App is not intended for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

10. International Data Transfers

Depending on the location of the third-party services we use, your information may be processed on servers located outside your country or region.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If we make material changes, we will notify you through the App or on our website.

12. Contact

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or the App's privacy practices, please contact us at:
banakai.support@gmail.com
