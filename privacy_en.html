Clopass Privacy Policy

Last Updated: July 11, 2026

Banakai ("we", "us", or "our") provides the mobile application "Clopass" (the "App"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect information in connection with the App.

1. Provider

Provider: Banakai
Contact: banakai.support@gmail.com

2. Information We Collect

The App processes the following information:

  • User Identifier: When you launch the App, an anonymous authentication session is created and an internal user ID is automatically issued. We do not normally collect your name, email address, or other information that directly identifies you.
  • User-generated Content: Notes, paragraphs, fill-in-the-blank settings, test results, learning history, stamp records, and other content you create, enter, paste, or import using the camera.
  • Camera Images: When you use the OCR (image-to-text) feature, captured images are sent to an AI service provider for OCR processing and processed temporarily. We do not store the images themselves on our servers.
  • Temporary Device Transfer Information: Temporary transfer codes and passwords used when moving your data to another device. They become invalid after expiration or once used.
  • Usage Counts: Usage counts for features such as camera OCR in order to manage free usage limits.
  • Technical Information: Server error logs and operational information used for debugging. These do not contain information that directly identifies you.

The App does not use advertising SDKs or analytics SDKs.

3. How We Use Information

  • To provide App features such as note management, tests, learning history, and device transfer.
  • To investigate and resolve technical issues.
  • To prevent abuse and manage usage limits.
  • To respond to inquiries from users.

4. Camera and Images

When you use the OCR feature, captured images are transmitted to an AI service provider for OCR processing. We do not store the images themselves. Only the text extracted through OCR is saved as part of your notes. The handling of data by third-party services is governed by the respective privacy policies of those service providers.

5. Third Parties

We use the following categories of third-party services as necessary to provide the App.

  • Cloud infrastructure, authentication, and database service providers (for storing user data and authentication).
  • AI service providers that perform OCR processing (for extracting text from images).

Except where required by law, we do not provide user information to third parties.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

Data you create within the App is retained until you delete it.

Codes and temporary information issued for device transfer become invalid after expiration or once used.

Codes generated for note sharing remain valid until you generate a new sharing code.

If you have questions regarding deletion of your data, please contact us. Depending on identity verification or technical limitations, we may not be able to fulfill every request.

7. Your Rights

You may contact us regarding deletion of your data. Please contact us using the information below.

8. Security

We implement reasonable and appropriate safeguards to protect the information we collect.

9. Children's Privacy

The App is not intended for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

10. International Data Transfers

Depending on the location of the third-party services we use, your information may be processed on servers located outside your country or region.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If material changes are made, we will notify users within the App or through our website.

12. Contact

If you have questions regarding this Privacy Policy or the privacy practices of the App, please contact:
banakai.support@gmail.com
