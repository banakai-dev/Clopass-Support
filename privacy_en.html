Clopass Privacy Policy

Banakai ("we", "us", or "our") provides the mobile application "Clopass" (the "App"). This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information in connection with the App.

1. Provider

Provider: Banakai
Contact: banakai.support@gmail.com

2. Information We Collect

  • Account identifier: When you start the App, an anonymous authentication session is created and an internal user ID is issued. We do not normally collect your name or email address.
  • User-generated content: Notes, paragraphs, fill-in-the-blank settings, test results, learning history, and related records that you create, enter, paste, or import via the camera.
  • Camera images: When you use the OCR (image-to-text) feature, captured images are processed only to extract text. We do not store the images themselves on our servers.
  • Temporary device handoff data: Short-lived codes and temporary passwords used to transfer your data to another device. They expire and are invalidated once used.
  • Usage counts: Counts of certain features (e.g. camera OCR) for free-tier management.
  • Technical information: Server error logs for debugging. These do not contain information that directly identifies you.

The App does not use advertising SDKs or analytics SDKs.

3. How We Use Information

  • To provide the App's features (note management, tests, learning history, device handoff, etc.)
  • To investigate and fix issues
  • To prevent abuse and manage usage limits
  • To respond to your inquiries

4. Camera and Images

When you use the OCR feature, we may send captured images to third-party AI service providers used to process OCR features. Images are used solely to extract text. Only the extracted text is saved as your note; the images themselves are not stored on our servers.

5. Third Parties

We use the following categories of third-party services as needed to operate the App. Specific vendors within each category may change over time.

  • Cloud infrastructure and authentication / database providers (for storing user data and managing sessions).
  • Third-party AI service providers used to process OCR features (for extracting text from images).

Except as required by law, we do not otherwise share your personal information with third parties.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

Data you create in the App is retained until you delete it or request account deletion. Temporary handoff data expires or is invalidated after single use.

7. Your Rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your data. Please contact us at the address below.

8. Security

We implement reasonable and appropriate measures to protect the information we collect. Database access is scoped so that each user can only access their own data.

9. Children's Privacy

The App is not primarily directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 without parental consent.

10. International Data Transfer

Depending on the location of the third-party services we use, your information may be processed on servers located outside your country of residence.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Policy from time to time. Material changes will be announced within the App or on our website.

12. Contact

For questions about this Policy or the App's privacy practices, please contact:
banakai.support@gmail.com
